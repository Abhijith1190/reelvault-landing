Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026  ·  Effective date: June 14, 2026

ReelVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ReelVault mobile application ("App").

By using ReelVault, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (when registering with email)
  • Display name
  • Profile picture URL (when signing in with Google)
  • Google account ID (when using Google Sign In)

Content You Save

  • URLs of videos you save (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and other platforms)
  • Titles and labels you provide for saved videos
  • AI-generated tags, categories, and summaries of your saved content
  • Thumbnail images associated with saved videos

Usage Information

  • Search queries you enter within the app
  • Categories and filters you use

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your ReelVault account
  • Store and organize your saved video content
  • Power AI categorization, tagging, and summarization of your saved videos
  • Enable semantic search across your saved content
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Improve the performance and features of the App
We do NOT sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. AI Processing

ReelVault uses AI services to automatically categorize, tag, and summarize your saved videos. Specifically:

  • The title and URL you provide are sent to AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq) for categorization
  • This processing happens securely and is used solely to organize your content
  • We use vector embeddings stored in our database to power semantic search
  • AI providers process your content according to their own privacy policies

Third-party AI providers we use:

  • Google Gemini — Google Privacy Policy
  • Groq — Groq Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on:

  • Supabase — PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS (Singapore region)
  • Railway — Application server hosted in US West region

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

5. Google Sign In

When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your display name
  • Your profile picture URL
  • A unique Google ID (used only for authentication)

We do not receive access to your Google contacts, Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google services.

6. Third-Party Services

ReelVault accesses publicly available metadata (titles, thumbnails) from video platforms when you save a URL. We access only public information — we do not access your accounts on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

Third-party services used by ReelVault:

  • Google Sign In (authentication)
  • Google Gemini API (AI categorization)
  • Groq API (AI fallback)
  • YouTube oEmbed API (video metadata)
  • Supabase (database)
  • Railway (hosting)

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at reelvaultapp@gmail.com.

When you delete a saved item within the app, it is permanently removed from our database immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — update incorrect information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and data
  • Export — request an export of your saved content
  • Opt-out — stop using the app at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at reelvaultapp@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

ReelVault is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • bcrypt password hashing
  • JWT tokens for secure authentication
  • Database access restricted to our application servers only

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
